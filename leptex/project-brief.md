# Project brief

Paste into **Overview → the description at the top of the project**.

This is the assistant's factual base. If it contradicts the website, the assistant
contradicts the website — so it must list everything the site shows.

> We're a small family farm in Redmond, WA. Over the season we grow cherries,
> blueberries, raspberries, green beans, figs, hawthorn, red-fleshed apples,
> Asian pears and persimmons — never more than a few at a time. Everything is
> hand-picked at peak ripeness and nothing is sprayed. We never store or ship:
> you order online and collect at the farm. Hawthorn is what we are featuring
> right now.

## Why this replaces the original

The first version of this brief said *"just three favourites this season: crisp
green beans, tart hawthorn berries, and our special red-fleshed apples."* The
site now shows six crops and the reply instructions name nine. A visitor who
clicks the cherry card and is told the farm only grows three things has been
handed a contradiction on their first message.

**Rule: the brief is a superset of what the site shows.** Never the other way round.
